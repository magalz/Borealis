Borealis streams games, programs or your full desktop from your PC to your Android phone, tablet or TV. It is a client based on <a href="https://github.com/moonlight-stream/moonlight-android">Moonlight</a>, tuned for the <a href="https://github.com/NortheBridge/luminalshine">LuminalShine</a> host. It may also work with other hosts that speak the same protocol, such as <a href="https://github.com/LizardByte/Sunshine">Sunshine</a>, but only LuminalShine is tested.

Borealis connects only to the host you choose. It has no ads, no in-app purchases and no analytics.

Streaming performance depends on your device and your network. HDR needs an HDR10-capable device, a host GPU that can encode HEVC Main 10 and a game with HDR10 enabled.

Features
* Free and open source (GPL-3.0)
* Streams games from any store, over your home network or the Internet
* Video up to 4K at 120 FPS with 7.1 surround sound and HDR
* H.264, HEVC and AV1 (AV1 needs a host GPU that can encode it)
* Keyboard, mouse, stylus and gamepad support, including Xbox and PlayStation controllers
* Local co-op with several controllers, force feedback and gamepad motion sensors
* Optional diagnostic bundle that you share yourself. It leaves your device only when you send it.

Getting started
1. Install and set up LuminalShine on your PC.
2. Open Borealis and tap your PC in the list. If it is not listed, add it by its address.
3. Borealis shows a PIN: enter it in the host's web interface.
4. Start streaming.

A mid-range or better wireless router with a 5 GHz connection to your device, and a wired connection from your PC to the router, give the best results.
